# Bundled assets

Piece sets and sounds are taken from [lichess.org/lila](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila)
(`public/piece/<set>`, `public/sound/<set>`); authors and licenses follow lila's
[COPYING.md](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md). Only sets with an open
license are bundled.
